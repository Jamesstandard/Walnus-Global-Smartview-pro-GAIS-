# SmartView-ai Module Catalog

SmartView Pro™ is structured into modular subsystems:

1. **SmartView-ai Copilot:** Conversational assistant, tool orchestrator, vector RAG knowledge base, and model selector (19 Mini SLMs).
2. **Camera & Capture System:** CameraX/MediaDevices capture with grid overlays, horizon stabilization, and image processing.
3. **HUD & Camera Guidance:** ADAS-inspired telemetry matrix with pitch/roll indicators, HDOP coordinates, and structural integrity meters.
4. **Device Capability Engine:** Automatic hardware capability detection and fallback handling.
5. **Project Management:** Project creation, details, and organization with Dexie offline-first storage.
6. **Survey Management:** Survey creation, site info, and capture workflow.
7. **Measurement System:** Distance, area, perimeter, and volume calculations with explicit data provenance.
8. **Paint Workflow:** Primer, base coat, topcoat, and finish calculation with custom color palettes and presets.
9. **Screeding & Plastering Workflows:** Polymer wall screening mortars and render stages.
10. **Material & Quantity Estimation:** Automated material planning, wastage allowances, and supplier pricing.
11. **Quotation & Reporting:** PDF/CSV export, quotation drafting, and client sharing.
12. **AI Agents & Background Services:** Multi-agent manager for automated quality control and estimation tasks.
13. **Settings & Configuration:** App preferences, model configuration, and permissions.
